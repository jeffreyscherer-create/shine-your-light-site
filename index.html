Taking care of yourself shouldn't feel like one more thing on your list.

Life is full. Between work, family, your health, and everything in between, taking care of yourself can easily become one more thing on an already long list. Even when you are ready to make a change, figuring out what works for you, your body and your life can feel overwhelming.

I understand that balancing act because I've lived it, too.

Health coaching gives you the space to focus on what matters most to you. Together, we'll turn your goals into realistic, sustainable habits that fit your life.

Our Mission

Shine Your Light is dedicated to empowering women to reclaim their health and vitality during life's most transformative seasons.

We understand the unique challenges that come with work-life balance, midlife, stress, perimenopause/menopause, and chronic health conditions. Our mission is to provide personalized health coaching that honors your individual journey while building sustainable habits that support lasting wellness.

True well-being comes from addressing the whole person—mind, body, and spirit. Health coaching gives you the space, support and accountability to identify what matters most, set realistic goals and make meaningful changes one step at a time.

Together, we will create positive changes around nutrition, movement, sleep, and stress management.
