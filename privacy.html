Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Shine Your Light LLC ("Shine Your Light," "we," "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website (shineyourlightcoach.com), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website and not to information you may share with us in person, by phone, or through other services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us directly, primarily through our contact form. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide it
  • Any message or details you include in the form

We do not currently use cookies, analytics software, or advertising trackers on this website. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Third-Party Services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser may connect directly to Google's servers, which can receive your IP address as part of that request. This is standard for most websites that use web fonts. We do not control how Google handles this data; you can review Google's privacy policy for more information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations or sessions, and communicate with you about our coaching and yoga services. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Please Don't Send Sensitive Health Details Through This Form

Our contact form is intended for general inquiries and scheduling — not for sharing detailed medical or health information. Shine Your Light LLC is a health and wellness coaching practice, not a medical provider, and is not a HIPAA-covered entity. Please save detailed health history and medical information for a private conversation once we've connected.

How We Store & Protect Your Information

Messages submitted through our contact form are delivered to our business email inbox. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing info@shineyourlightcoach.com.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
