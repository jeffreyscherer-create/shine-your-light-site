About

My Story

I'm Cassie — certified yoga instructor and health & wellness coach. I help women make space for their health and well-being, find what works for their unique lives, and feel healthier, happier and more like themselves.

I began my health journey in my teenage years, struggling with ulcerative colitis and all that came with it for me—hospital stays, ER visits, surgeries, medications, and stroke. For years, I tried to outrun my health issues, pretending I was fine. I built a career as an attorney, working around the clock while balancing my family, my health, and everything else that needed my attention. I believed that if I worked hard enough and did things "right," everything else would fall into place. For years, my body, my happiness, my health, came in last place.

I know what it's like to juggle work, family and your health—and how lonely and scary it can sometimes feel to navigate the healthcare system. I've been through the ups and downs, setbacks and plenty of uncertainty. And I've learned firsthand that sometimes you can do everything "right" and your body still has other plans.

My high-achieving mindset followed me into my health, too: constant striving, all-or-nothing thinking and very little room for grace. It wasn't until a particularly demanding season of my life that I began to look at my well-being differently and discovered health coaching.

I realized that feeling better isn't about doing everything perfectly. It's about understanding yourself, figuring out what works for your body and your life, building habits you can actually sustain, and having someone in your corner along the way.

Today, I help women navigate the balancing act I know so well—careers, families, health challenges, stress and the desire to make space for themselves again. I want to offer the kind of support that can be so hard to find: someone to listen, help you sort through the noise, and work alongside you as you build a healthier, happier life that works for you.
